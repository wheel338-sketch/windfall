## `fx-fruity-send` — Send Tap

Summary: "Taps the signal at its position in the effect chain and sends it to
another mixer track."

Claim: implemented end to end; **full-row candidate once the parent's runs
pass**. Engine and project behaviour ran green before the rule; the inspector
UI and its mounted tests are written, not executed.

What was built (shared with `fx-transmitter`):

- Model: `EffectOutputRoute { effect, output, target }` in
  `Mixer.effect_outputs` (`crates/windfall-project/src/effect_outputs.rs`).
  Routes are keyed by effect id, so they follow an effect along its chain.
- Command `SetEffectOutput { effect, output, target? }`: one undo step; refused
  for an output the effect lacks, an effect on the master or Current, its own
  track, a missing or Current target, and any route that would loop. Outputs,
  sends and sidechains are refused the other way round too, because routes are
  now part of the mixer's routing graph.
- Pruning in the same undo step: deleting or replacing the effect, deleting
  the target or source track, loading a track preset over the chain, or moving
  the effect to where its route would be invalid or loop.
- Engine: a route is a plan edge that leaves from its effect's place in the
  chain, before the fader (`PlanEdge.tap`). It has its own compensation delay
  (`DelayKey::Tap`), counted from the latency of the effects up to and
  including its own, so the copy and the track's own output meet on one frame.
  It takes part in routing order and solo, and follows the source's mute and
  solo but not its fader or pan.
- DSP: `SendTap::send_frames` glides to a changed level over 5 ms.
  `EffectSlot::process_routed` scales the sent signal by the slot's switch and
  mix, and the rack scales it with the effect's fade in and out.
- Stems: a track fed only by a routed output counts as a source in
  "through" mode.
- UI: `apps/desktop/src/features/mixer/effect-outputs.tsx`, shown under the
  effect's editor in the inspector. One destination menu per output, loop-aware,
  with "Not routed"; a plain explanation on the master.

Tests:
