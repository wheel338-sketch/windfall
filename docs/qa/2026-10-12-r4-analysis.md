## Shared memory contract

`crates/windfall-analysis/src/classical_memory.rs` supplies checked aggregate
preflight bounds; `work.rs` enforces the default 256-MiB classical quota and
exposes `peak_memory_bytes`. Denoise charges simultaneous f64 sample
accumulators, f64 frame weights and returned f32 PCM. Rendering charges the
denoise stage and largest contextual stretch stage, including interleaved
interval, planar copy/growth, interval output, full final output and seam
storage. Detection charges FFT/YIN preparation, estimates/segments,
transients/notes/counts/candidates and retained results. Neutral paths preserve
their cheap shared-buffer bypass.

`crates/windfall-core/src/lib.rs::AudioBuffer::retained_bytes` includes unused
sample capacity. Native `audio_editor/classical_editor.rs` adds all distinct
prepared audio allocations, actual recipe capacities, incoming edit/selection
capacities and still-live controller auditions. Refusals preserve the old
proposal and document/history. `crates/windfall-engine/src/preview_memory.rs`,
registered in `lib.rs` and used by `controller.rs`, records weak identities in
256 fixed slots. Pending messages, playing previews and garbage waiting for
control retirement stay charged until the actual allocation dies. The table
does not pin PCM or add callback bookkeeping. Exhaustion fails analysis
admission closed; other surviving owners may conservatively overcharge it.
