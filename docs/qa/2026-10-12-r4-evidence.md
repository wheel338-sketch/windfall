must preserve document/history and return no partial MediaRenderAudio.

guard and region. The mounted test enables the native-only action, supplies

and clicks **Export synchronized AVI**. It runs the real rasterizer, AVI writer
and Blob/download publication. Only the download destination is adapted to a
test-owned filesystem directory. A separate RIFF reader parses those written

changing video pixels, exact 24,025-frame audio length, the 25-frame final audio
chunk, every quantized stereo sample, and every index offset/length. It also
checks retained song phase and metadata. A second action is cancelled at the
encoder's real frame-eight rendering-lifecycle yield; no new Blob/file/metadata
may publish and the previously accepted AVI must remain byte-identical.
