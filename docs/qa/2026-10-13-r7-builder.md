### Step 1: the two named devices (committed)

**Multiband delay.** `BandDelayParams` gained a layout choice (three bands as
before, or sixteen), an active bank (1 to 8) and eight banks of sixteen delay,
level and pan values: 386 parameters appended after the existing fifteen, so
saved indices 0 to 14 are unchanged and a project saved before this opens as
the three-band layout with every new field at its default. The three-band
path runs the same arithmetic as before. The sixteen-band path splits at
fifteen fixed edges from 40 Hz to 16 kHz in equal ratios (Windfall's choice:
the public definition gives no band frequencies), each band the difference of
two neighbouring two-section one-pole lowpasses so the bands still sum to the
input. Its bands share the three feedback controls by where their centre falls
against the two crossover controls. A layout change fades the echoes out over
5 ms, clears them and fades the new layout in.

Import: every `bank_N/{delay,vol,pan}.M` float lands on
`banks.{N-1}.{timeMs,level,pan}.M`. Scaling: the raw float is taken in the
stand-in's own unit (milliseconds, gain, pan) and clamped to its range
(1..1000, 0..1, -1..1), because the public definition names the arrays and
gives no unit. The layout is set to sixteen bands and bank 1 plays, because
the public definition names no playing bank; feedback and mix stay at the
stand-in defaults because it names neither. The report keeps one line per bank
and row with every source name, value and target.

One existing expectation changed, in `round6_native.rs`: it read bank 1 band 0
from `timeMs.0`, the legacy "low" control of the three-band layout. That
control is not what the field describes; it now reads `banks.0.timeMs.0`. The
value asserted (120) is unchanged.

**Three-oscillator synth (SimSynth).** `TripleOscParams` gained, appended after
index 19: a pulse width and two octave-layer switches per oscillator, and the
level of the filter's band-pass and high-pass outputs. With the new fields at
their defaults the output is sample-identical to before (tested against a
project saved in the old shape).

- `osc*_pw`: DawVert `wave.py` draws its square high where the sine exceeds
  `(pw - 0.5) * 2`, which is a share `0.5 - asin((pw - 0.5) * 2) / pi` of the
  cycle. That share is the stand-in's pulse width; a square with a width other
  than half becomes the pulse waveform. Other shapes carry the value unheard,
  as in the public converter.
- `osc*_o1`, `osc*_o2`: DawVert adds the same wave at twice and four times the
  pitch, each mixed half and half with what is already there. The two switches
  do exactly that.
- `svf_band`, `svf_high`: no public rule; the 0..1 value is the level of that
  filter output beside the low-pass (band at unity peak gain). A switched-off
  source filter sets both to 0.
- `lfo_del`, `lfo_retrigger`: the channel LFOs gained a start delay and a
  retrigger switch (`TargetLfo.delay_ms`, `.retrigger`, both defaulted for old
  projects; the engine holds the wave out for the delay and fades it in over
  5 ms; retriggered, the wave waits at the start of its cycle). The public
  converter reads `lfo_del` without giving it a rule, so the delay uses the
  time rule the same device publishes for its envelopes, `4 * v^3` seconds.
  The channel-level LFO's public retrigger flag (PyFLP `LFOPhaseRetrig`,
  bit 5) now maps to the same switch.

Two expectations changed for the new fields: the parameter counts in

unchanged), and one whole-object comparison of a default LFO in
`voice-panel.test.tsx`, which now lists the two new fields at their defaults.
