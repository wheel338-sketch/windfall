## Current application contract � 2026-10-11

The historical foundation reference below remains load-compatible. The full
application workflows now use six additional append-only kinds:

| Row | Current kind / product | Prepared contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| inst-harmless | HarmonicFilter / Harmonic Filter | Immutable 64 harmonics with saw/square/triangle weighting and spectral cutoff/resonance |
| inst-morphine | SnapshotMorph / Snapshot Morph | Worker-prepared A/B harmonic snapshots, amplitude/phase/pan interpolation and harmonic editor |
| inst-ogun | MetalVoice / Metal Voice | Inharmonic partials, stretch, independent shimmer and finite decay |
| inst-harmor | SpectralResynth / Spectral Resynth | Worker harmonic WAV projection or image luminance frames, manual freeze or timed scan |

| inst-beepmap | ColorScan / Color Scan | Image rows/hue to frequency, squared luminance/alpha to amplitude, red/blue to pan; timed scan |

`InstrumentAsset::Spectra` contains non-Copy SpectralBank/Frame/Partial data.
Copy params contain scalar controls only. Preparation happens off audio;
process/events/reset read immutable assets and fixed oscillator/envelope state.
There are at most 16 voices and 64 partials, with 5 ms control smoothing,
4 ms steal/stop continuity, shortest-path phase interpolation, Nyquist fading
and stereo output. Scan interpolates back to its first frame at loop boundaries.
Banks hold at most 256 frames. WAV and browser image source files cap at 16 MiB;
WAV preparation retains at most 32768 mono frames. Harmonic projection omits DC
and noisy residuals; exact arbitrary recording reconstruction is not promised.

ChannelSource and instrument plugin presets persist optional assets; checked
SetInstrumentAsset captures the complete expected source, rejects stale/invalid
results, and supplies ordinary history. Plans build changed assets into fresh
units off audio and retire old units on the control side. Cancellation covers
project replacement, history navigation, unmount and worker timeout.

Registry, descriptors, project persistence, engine hosting, purpose-built
channel-rack editors and acceptance sources are implemented. All new acceptance
is **written, not executed**. See [current hand-off](../../qa/2026-10-11-inst-additive-physical-drum.md)
for exact tests/regeneration/risks. The following sections describe the earlier
six foundation kinds, not this current application contract.

## Public types

Instruments, each implementing `Instrument`:

- `additive::HarmonicStack`
- `additive::PartialMorph`
- `additive::Inharmonic`
- `additive::Resynth`
- `additive::SeedPatch`
- `additive::ScanSynth`

Parameter structs, each deriving `Serialize`, `Deserialize`, `TS`, `Debug`,
`Clone`, `Copy`, `PartialEq` and a hand-written `ParamSet`, with
`#[serde(rename_all = "camelCase", default)]` and `#[ts(export)]` the way
`balance.rs` does:

- `additive::HarmonicStackParams` — `ParamSet::NAME` is `"Harmonic stack"`, 75 controls
- `additive::PartialMorphParams` — `"Partial morph"`, 74 controls
- `additive::InharmonicParams` — `"Inharmonic"`, 12 controls
- `additive::ResynthParams` — `"Resynth"`, 106 controls
- `additive::SeedPatchParams` — `"Seed patch"`, 12 controls
- `additive::ScanSynthParams` — `"Scan synth"`, 42 controls

Supporting parameter types, also exported to TypeScript:

- `additive::VoicingParams` — envelope, polyphony, velocity, volume, pan;
  embedded as `voicing` in all six. Its `envelope` is the existing
  `windfall_dsp::EnvelopeParams`, reused rather than redefined.
- `additive::TablePartial` — one row of the `Resynth` table: `ratio`,
  `gain`, `phase`
- `additive::PartialGains` — the 64 levels of `HarmonicStack`. A newtype
  rather than `[f32; 64]` because `serde` only describes arrays up to 32
  entries; see [Wire format](#wire-format).

Constants:

- `additive::MAX_PARTIALS` — 64, the engine's bank width
- `additive::MAX_POLYPHONY` — 16, the largest `voicing.polyphony`
- `additive::MORPH_PARTIALS` — 32, the width of each morph snapshot
- `additive::TABLE_PARTIALS` — 32, the rows of the `Resynth` table
- `additive::SCAN_STEPS` — 32, the values in the `ScanSynth` row
- `additive::MAX_SEED` — 16777215, the largest addressable `SeedPatch` seed
- `additive::GLIDE_MS` — 5.0, the time a changed control takes

`lib.rs` was not edited, so these are reached as
`windfall_dsp::additive::HarmonicStack` and so on. Adding root re-exports

## What makes each one distinct

The six are not variants of one instrument. Five are banks of sines and
differ in where the frequencies, levels and phases come from; the sixth is
not additive at all.

| Instrument | Frequencies | Levels | Phases |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `HarmonicStack` | `n`, always | one control each, times a tilt and a rolloff | all zero |
| `PartialMorph` | `n`, always | interpolated between two snapshots by one control | all zero |
| `Inharmonic` | `n^(1+stretch) * sqrt(1 + B n^2)`, normalised so the lowest is 1 | a rolloff only; no per-partial control | all zero |
| `Resynth` | a control each, times a stretch | a control each | a control each |
| `SeedPatch` | drawn from a seed | drawn from a seed, times a rolloff | all zero |
| `ScanSynth` | — the row is the waveform, not a spectrum | — | — |

`PartialMorph` is not `HarmonicStack` with extra steps: the spectrum it
plays is in neither of its snapshots, and the control that matters is not a
level. `Inharmonic` has no per-partial controls at all. `SeedPatch` has no
spectrum in its parameters. Tests assert all of this, including that the
six put out six measurably different signals at their defaults
(`the_six_instruments_are_six_sounds`).

## Descriptor layout

`VoicingParams` is the last eight rows of every table, always in this
order:

| Offset | Id | Kind | Range | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| +0 | `voicing.envelope.attackMs` | float | 0..10000 | 5 |
| +1 | `voicing.envelope.decayMs` | float, log | 1..10000 | 400 |
| +2 | `voicing.envelope.sustain` | float | 0..1 | 0.7 |
| +3 | `voicing.envelope.releaseMs` | float, log | 1..10000 | 300 |
| +4 | `voicing.polyphony` | integer | 1..16 | 8 |
| +5 | `voicing.velocity` | float | 0..1 | 0.7 |
| +6 | `voicing.gain` | float | 0..2 | 0.2 |
| +7 | `voicing.pan` | float | -1..1 | 0 |

Before that, each instrument's own rows:

- **HarmonicStack** (75): `0` `partials` (int 1..64, 16), `1` `oddEvenTilt`
  (-1..1, 0), `2` `rolloff` (0..1, 0), `3..=66` `gains.0`..`gains.63`
  (0..1, defaulting to `1/n`), `67..=74` voicing.
- **PartialMorph** (74): `0` `partials` (int 1..32, 24), `1` `morph`
  (0..1, 0), `2..=33` `snapshotA.0`..`snapshotA.31` (default: the
  fundamental alone), `34..=65` `snapshotB.0`..`snapshotB.31` (default:
  `1/n` on the odd partials, silence on the even), `66..=73` voicing.
- **Inharmonic** (12): `0` `partials` (int 1..64, 24), `1` `stiffness`
  (0..1, 0.35), `2` `stretch` (0.02..0.3, 0.08), `3` `rolloff` (0..1, 0.3),
  `4..=11` voicing.
- **Resynth** (106): `0` `partials` (int 1..32, 16), `1` `stretch`
  (0.5..2 log, 1), then row `i` at `2 + 3*i` in ratio, level, phase order:
  `table.i.ratio` (0.25..64 log, default `i+1`), `table.i.gain` (0..1,
  default `1/(i+1)`), `table.i.phase` (0..1, default `(i % 8) / 8`).
  `98..=105` voicing.
- **SeedPatch** (12): `0` `seed` (int 0..16777215, 12345), `1` `partials`
  (int 1..64, 32), `2` `spread` (0..1, 0.3), `3` `rolloff` (0..1, 0.3),
  `4..=11` voicing.
- **ScanSynth** (42): `0..=31` `row.0`..`row.31` (-1..1, defaulting to a
  32-step ramp), `32` `interpolate` (toggle, off), `33` `smoothing`
  (0..1, 0), `34..=41` voicing.

Indices are stable within this version of each instrument, which is what
`ParamSet` promises. `index_of`, `get`, `set`, `sanitized` and `approach`
all work off these tables; `set` past the end returns false and changes
nothing. Every table is checked against its struct, its `Default`, its
serde names and its JSON by `every_parameter_table_agrees_with_its_struct`.

Nothing here is a `Choice`, so the parent's UI needs no new choice
handling. `ScanSynth`'s `interpolate` is the only toggle.

## Wire format

Every struct is `#[serde(default)]`, so a missing field is its default and
`{}` deserialises to `Default::default()`.

`PartialGains` is a newtype over `[f32; 64]` with hand-written `Serialize`
and `Deserialize`, because `serde`'s array impls stop at 32 entries. On the
wire it is a plain JSON array; `ts_rs` exports it as a 64-element tuple
type, since its `array_tuple_limit` is 64. **A short array loads, leaving
the partials past its end silent** — which is how a project written before
the bank was this wide, or a damaged one, survives. A long one is truncated.
This is the only place in the module where array length is forgiving;
`[f32; 32]` and `[TablePartial; 32]` use serde's own impls and reject a
wrong length.

`SeedPatch::seed` is a `u32` on the wire, but its control range stops at
`MAX_SEED` (2^24 - 1) because `ParamSet` reads and writes `f32` and that is
the largest integer an `f32` carries exactly. A seed above it is clamped by
`sanitized`, so **the parent should not offer the full `u32` range in the
UI or in a preset format**, or a seed will change when a project is opened
and saved.

## Realtime behaviour

Every voice, table, ramp and bank is a fixed-size inline array. Building an
instrument and `prepare` allocate nothing either. The five sine-bank
instruments are about 8.8 KB by value and `ScanSynth` about 2.3 KB, so the
parent will want them behind the `Box` that `AnyInstrument` already uses; a
test holds the ceiling at 16 KB.

`process`, `set_params`, `reset`, `set_tempo`, `note_on`, `note_off` and
`all_notes_off` never allocate, free, lock, wait or do IO. `set_params`
fills a `Spectrum` on the stack and hands it to the engine; that costs up to
64 `powf` calls, which is arithmetic, not a syscall. No randomness, analysis
or decoding happens in `process` — `SeedPatch` draws its spectrum in
`set_params`, and the same seed gives the same spectrum on every machine,
sample rate and run.

- **Latency** is zero and there is **no tail**: when no voice is active the
  output is exact zero, so `tail_samples` is 0 and `active_voices` reaching
  0 means silence. Tested.
- **Block size** does not change the output. The control grid is counted in
  samples, and a `Tone::render` is told how far into the period it starts,
  so a period split across two blocks renders the same as one that is not.
  `every_instrument_ignores_the_block_size` compares block sizes 1, 2, 3, 7,
  16, 17, 64 and 333 against 512 across note-on, a second note-on and a
  note-off, to within 1e-6.
- **Glide** is 5 ms (`GLIDE_MS`), linear, per sample, on every partial
  level, every row value and the output gain. Partial ratios and the two
  `ScanSynth` reading modes change on the 16-sample control boundary
  instead, because they are retunings rather than levels; a partial on its
  way out keeps the pitch it had so that lowering `partials` does not slide
  it. Changes made before the first block after `prepare` or `reset` snap.
- **Notes** are keys 0..127 and velocities. A velocity that is zero,
  negative or not a number is a note-off. `voicing.velocity` says how much
  a soft note is quieter; at velocity 1 a note is at full level whatever it
  is set to, which is what the measurement tests rely on.
- **Polyphony** is `voicing.polyphony`, 1..16, with four spare voices
  behind it. A note past the limit takes over from the oldest released
  voice, or the oldest held one, and the voice it takes is faded out over
  4 ms in a spare, so stealing does not click. `all_notes_off` uses the same
  fade. `reset` silences everything at once.
- **Levels add.** There is no normalising and no limiter: 64 partials at
  full level and 16 notes at once will go far past unity on purpose.
  `voicing.gain` defaults to 0.2 and the default spectra fall away as
  `1/n`, which leaves room for a chord, but a deliberately loud patch is
  the user's to manage. Output is still guarded: anything outside ±1000 or
  not a number becomes zero, so a damaged project cannot produce `NaN`
  downstream (`every_instrument_is_silent_and_finite`).
- **Anti-aliasing.** A partial fades out between 0.44 and 0.49 cycles per
  sample and is silent above that, so pitch changes do not click as
  partials cross Nyquist, and a note whose fundamental is above Nyquist is
  silent. `ScanSynth` is deliberately **not** band-limited: a staircase has
  corners sharper than the rate can carry and what folds back is the sound.
  Its `smoothing` control is the way to take that off.
- **Sample rates** clamp to 1..768000 Hz; a rate that is not a number falls
  back to 48000.
- **Tempo** does nothing: nothing here is tempo-synced.

The sine bank uses a seventh-order minimax polynomial on the quarter cycle
rather than `f32::sin`, because a full bank at full polyphony is about 1300
sines per output sample. Its worst-case error against `f32::sin` is 1.1e-6,
about -119 dB, and is tested
(`the_polynomial_sine_matches_the_library_one`). Cost is the thing to watch
on this module: one sine per partial per sample per voice, so 64 partials
and 16 notes is roughly twenty times what the subtractive synth costs for
one note. `voicing.polyphony` and `partials` are the two controls that
decide it, and the parent may want the UI to say so.

## What is not here

- **No image resynthesis.** `Resynth` plays a table that is already in the
  project. It does not read an audio file or an image, and there is no
  spectral analysis, FFT reconstruction or time-varying resynthesis.
  Whatever writes the table — a preset, an editor, an analysis pass added
  later — is outside this module. `inst-harmor` is therefore only partly
  covered.
- **No image mapping in `ScanSynth`** either. The row is 32 numbers in the
  project; nothing maps colour or brightness to frequency. `inst-beepmap`
  is likewise only partly covered.
- **No filter, LFO or modulation matrix** in any of the six, and no unison.
  What the partials say is what is heard.
- **No per-note expression.** `supports_note_instances` is false, so
  `note_on_expression`, `note_on_instance`, `set_note_expression` and
  `set_note_pitch` fall through to the trait's key-based defaults. Per-note
  pitch, pan and expression would need per-voice state the engine does not
  keep yet; the place to add it is `Voice` in `engine.rs`.
- **No preset content.** The defaults are plain shapes (a saw spectrum, a
  sine against a hollow square, a 32-step ramp) chosen so that each
  instrument makes a sound out of the box and so that the six are audibly
  different from one another.

## Verifying this module

```sh
# From Git Bash on Windows
source scripts/msvc-env.sh
export TS_RS_EXPORT_DIR="$(mktemp -d)"
cargo test -p windfall-dsp --lib additive
```

40 tests pass: 31 measurements and nine `ts_rs` export tests. The whole
crate's library tests pass as well, and `cargo clippy -p windfall-dsp
--lib --all-targets` reports nothing in `src/additive`. The module was
formatted with `rustfmt --edition 2024 crates/windfall-dsp/src/additive/*.rs`
rather than `cargo fmt -p windfall-dsp`, which would also have reformatted
`examples/descriptors.rs` outside this seam.

What the measurements cover:

- Harmonic levels on a long steady note match the levels asked for, to
  within 2%, measured by Goertzel at 44 kHz where A4 is exactly 100 samples
  a cycle, so every harmonic lands on a bin and nothing leaks between them.
- The odd/even tilt silences exactly one half of the harmonics; the rolloff
  halves the second harmonic and quarters the fourth.
- Morph at 0 and at 1 reproduce the two snapshots exactly, both in the
  levels handed to the engine and in the measured output; 0.5 is halfway
  partial by partial; the sweep is monotonic in both directions at once; and
  the two ends are measurably different sounds.
- No `Inharmonic` partial above the lowest is within 2% of the harmonic it
  would otherwise be, at every corner of the stiffness and stretch ranges,
  and the series always climbs. A default note has no energy at its own
  octave.
- `Resynth` sounds a table of non-integer ratios at the levels given, with
  nothing at the harmonics the table does not hold; the phase column
  changes the waveform while leaving the spectrum alone; the stretch moves
  the whole table.
- The same `SeedPatch` seed gives bit-identical output and an identical
  spectrum; a different seed gives neither; raising `partials` leaves the
  partials below untouched; a spread of 0 leaves the series harmonic.
- `ScanSynth` at a rate where A4 is 128 samples a cycle reproduces the row
  sample for sample, four samples a step, and repeats with the row's
  period. Interpolating rounds the corners without moving the period.
  Smoothing cuts the staircase's step to under a quarter without silencing
  the note.
- All six: block-size invariance, exact silence with no notes, finite
  output with every control driven to `NaN`, ±infinity and ±1e9, bounded
  polyphony under 60 simultaneous notes, no step when a control moves
  mid-note, a note-on at zero velocity releasing the note, and `reset`
  silencing at once.
- Every parameter table against its struct: no blank rows, no duplicate
  ids, defaults inside their ranges and equal to the struct's `Default`,
  positive floors on logarithmic scales, each JSON path resolving to the
  right value, writing one control changing only that control, `sanitized`
  being idempotent and fixing damage, `approach` arriving exactly, and a
  JSON round trip.
